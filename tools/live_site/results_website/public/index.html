Sidewalk robot - SLAM project (Mitacs)

SLAM (building a map while working out where you are in it) with a ZED X stereo camera on a Husky robot. Three pages, one link key:
Computer statusThe Jetson (camera computer) and the robot's own computer: memory, processor, temperatures, every disk including the USB stick and the robot's card, and the robot's battery voltage. Live, every 2 s. Live mapThe floor map the camera is building during a drive, the drive's status lines, and the robot's LiDAR view. Live, every 2 s. ResultsEach finished drive: headline numbers, figures and timelapse videos. Updated after each drive.
All times are Hamilton time (America/Toronto). The Jetson sends outward to this website; nothing on the lab network is opened.
