Results - one pack per drive

Numbers, figures and timelapse videos from each finished drive and rehearsal. Updated after each drive, not live.
How to read these: the LiDAR (a laser scanner on the robot) is a second opinion, not ground truth (the exact true path) - figures say "agreement with the LiDAR estimate". "Tracking alone" = where the camera thought it was without any map corrections; "corrected" = after the map recognised places it had seen and pulled itself straight (loop closures). Lines: robot / LiDAR solid, camera and blend dashed.
